Topic: The role of water and hydration
Last reviewed: not yet reviewed (drafted 2026-09-07 as part of nutrition-assistant-service's implementation-plan resolution 2 -- a small, hand-authored, non-controversial seed corpus)

Water is essential for many bodily functions, including regulating body temperature, transporting nutrients, and supporting digestion. The body loses water through sweat, urine, and breathing, and replaces it through drinking fluids and eating water-containing foods. Individual fluid needs vary based on factors like activity level, climate, and overall health.
