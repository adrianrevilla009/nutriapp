A balanced meal generally refers to a meal that includes a combination of food groups -- such as a source of protein, a source of carbohydrates (often including fiber-rich options like vegetables or whole grains), and some fat -- rather than relying heavily on a single food group. What counts as "balanced" can vary based on individual needs, preferences, and cultural food patterns, and is a general concept rather than a fixed formula.
