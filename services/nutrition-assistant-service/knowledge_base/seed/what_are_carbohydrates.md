Topic: What are carbohydrates
Last reviewed: not yet reviewed (drafted 2026-09-07 as part of nutrition-assistant-service's implementation-plan resolution 2 -- a small, hand-authored, non-controversial seed corpus)

Carbohydrates are a macronutrient found in foods such as grains, fruits, vegetables, and legumes, and are the body's primary source of quick energy. Carbohydrates are generally categorized as sugars, starches, and fiber. During digestion, most carbohydrates are broken down into glucose, which the body uses for energy or stores for later use.
