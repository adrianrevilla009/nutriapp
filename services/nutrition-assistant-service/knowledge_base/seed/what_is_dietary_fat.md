Dietary fat is a macronutrient that provides a concentrated source of energy, supports the absorption of fat-soluble vitamins (such as vitamins A, D, E, and K), and is a component of cell membranes and hormones. Common food sources include oils, nuts, seeds, avocado, dairy, and animal products. Fats are often described by their chemical structure as saturated or unsaturated.
