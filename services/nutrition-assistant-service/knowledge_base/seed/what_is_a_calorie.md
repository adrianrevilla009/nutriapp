Topic: What is a calorie
Last reviewed: not yet reviewed (drafted 2026-09-07 as part of nutrition-assistant-service's implementation-plan resolution 2 -- a small, hand-authored, non-controversial seed corpus)

A calorie (specifically a kilocalorie, or kcal, which is what food labels usually mean by "Calorie") is a unit used to measure the amount of energy that food provides to the body, and the amount of energy the body uses for basic functions and physical activity. Foods and drinks vary widely in how many calories they contain per serving, largely based on their content of carbohydrates, protein, fat, and alcohol.
