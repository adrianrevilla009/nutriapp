Topic: What is protein
Last reviewed: not yet reviewed (drafted 2026-09-07 as part of nutrition-assistant-service's implementation-plan resolution 2 -- a small, hand-authored, non-controversial seed corpus)

Protein is a macronutrient made up of amino acids, which the body uses to build and repair tissues such as muscle, skin, and organs, and to produce enzymes and hormones. Protein is found in both animal sources (such as meat, fish, eggs, and dairy) and plant sources (such as beans, lentils, nuts, and soy).
