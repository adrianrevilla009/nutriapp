Topic: What is fiber
Last reviewed: not yet reviewed (drafted 2026-09-07 as part of nutrition-assistant-service's implementation-plan resolution 2 -- a small, hand-authored, non-controversial seed corpus)

Fiber is a type of carbohydrate found in plant foods (such as vegetables, fruits, whole grains, and legumes) that the body cannot fully digest. Unlike other carbohydrates, fiber passes through the digestive system largely intact, which supports regular digestion. Fiber is commonly grouped into soluble and insoluble types, both of which are found in many whole plant foods.
