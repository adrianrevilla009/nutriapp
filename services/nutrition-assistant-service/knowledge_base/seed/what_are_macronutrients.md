Topic: What are macronutrients
Last reviewed: not yet reviewed (drafted 2026-09-07 as part of nutrition-assistant-service's implementation-plan resolution 2 -- a small, hand-authored, non-controversial seed corpus)

Macronutrients are the nutrients the body needs in relatively large amounts to function: carbohydrates, protein, and fat. Each provides energy (measured in calories) and plays distinct roles -- carbohydrates are a primary energy source, protein supports building and repairing tissues, and fat supports energy storage, hormone production, and absorption of certain vitamins. A varied diet typically includes all three in some proportion.
